## Problem

A team needs one place to plan tasks and move them through stages without losing track of who does what.

## My role

I owned the contact management feature: creating, editing, deleting and grouping contacts, the detail views and the validation logic.

## How it works

Supabase handles authentication and PostgreSQL persistence. Angular CDK and RxJS power the drag and drop workflow and the reactive data handling.

[Live demo](https://join.kamycoding.com) and [source on GitHub](https://github.com/kamycoding/join-kanban).
