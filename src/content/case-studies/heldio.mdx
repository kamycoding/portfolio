## Problem

Most language courses teach the same path to everyone, whatever the learner actually needs the language for.

## Approach

The MVP covers the complete learning flow: capture a goal and personal context, ask relevant follow-up questions, create a learning plan, practice the right skills, give feedback and track progress.

## My role

I lead the product concept, MVP scope and roadmap, and work on the technical architecture and the frontend. The responsive web platform is built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, NestJS and PostgreSQL.

In development. Visit [heldio.app](https://heldio.app/).
